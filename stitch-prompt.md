Design a modern, minimalist, premium one-page website (desktop + mobile versions) for Marcin Rumianowski, an independent real estate agent in Warsaw, Poland, specialising in apartments. All on-page copy must be in Polish.

BRAND & STYLE
- Mood: calm, confident, premium, understated. Lots of white space, clean lines, large photography, restrained use of ornament.
- Palette: deep navy (#0F1F3D) as primary, warm gold (#B8975A) as the single accent, warm beige/ivory (#F6F1E7) as the soft background, white cards, dark text (#1A1A1A). No other accent colours.
- Typography: elegant serif for headlines (e.g. Playfair Display or Cormorant), clean sans-serif for body and UI (e.g. Inter). Generous line height.
- Shapes: subtle rounded corners (8-12px), thin 1px borders, very soft shadows. Gold used for buttons, small icons, thin dividers and rating stars.
- Photography: use placeholder images of Warsaw skyline at dusk, bright modern apartment interiors, and a professional portrait placeholder of a man in a suit.

PAGE STRUCTURE (top to bottom)
1. Sticky header: text logo "Marcin Rumianowski" with small subtitle "Pośrednik nieruchomości · Warszawa", anchor links (Oferty, Usługi, O mnie, Opinie, Kontakt), and a gold "Zadzwoń" button with a phone icon.
2. Hero: full-width large photo of Warsaw skyline / luxury apartment interior with a dark navy overlay. Headline in serif: "Nieruchomości w Warszawie. Bez kompromisów." Subline: "Sprzedaż, zakup i wynajem mieszkań z pełnym wsparciem od pierwszej rozmowy do przekazania kluczy." Two buttons: gold "Bezpłatna wycena" and outlined white "Zobacz oferty". Under the buttons, a slim row of 3 trust figures (e.g. "10+ lat doświadczenia", "200+ transakcji", "5,0 ★ w Google").
3. "Aktualne oferty" - horizontal carousel of apartment cards (arrows + swipe + dots). Each card: large photo, district badge (e.g. "Mokotów"), price, area in m², number of rooms, floor, a short title, and a small button "Zobacz na Otodom" / "Zobacz na Morizon" with the portal name as a subtle label. Show 3 cards on desktop, 1.2 on mobile. Below the carousel: link "Wszystkie oferty na Otodom i Morizon".
4. "Usługi" - 4 minimal cards with thin line icons: Sprzedaż mieszkania, Zakup mieszkania, Wynajem, Wycena nieruchomości. One short sentence each.
5. "Jak pracuję" - 4-step horizontal timeline with numbers in gold (Rozmowa, Strategia i wycena, Prezentacja i negocjacje, Finalizacja).
6. "O mnie" - split layout: portrait photo on one side, short biography on the other (3-4 sentences, calm expert tone), list of credentials (licencja pośrednika, ubezpieczenie OC, lata na rynku) and a signature-style detail.
7. "Opinie klientów" - carousel of Google reviews on beige background. Each card: reviewer name and avatar initial, 5 gold stars, review text (3-4 lines), date, small Google "G" logo. Above the carousel: overall rating "5,0 ★ · X opinii w Google" and a button "Zobacz wszystkie opinie w Google".
8. "Bezpłatna wycena" - navy section with a short form (imię, telefon, adres nieruchomości, wiadomość) and a gold submit button "Wyślij zapytanie". Next to the form: quick contact options (phone, WhatsApp, e-mail).
9. Footer: navy, logo, phone, e-mail, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram icons, "Warszawa i okolice", copyright and privacy policy link.

REQUIREMENTS
- Clean, high-end real-estate feel, not a template-looking portal. Avoid clutter, stock icons overload, gradients and neon.
- Mobile: single column, sticky bottom bar with two buttons "Zadzwoń" and "WhatsApp".
- Show clear hover/focus states and accessible contrast (WCAG AA).
- Deliver both a desktop (1440px) and a mobile (390px) version of the full page.
